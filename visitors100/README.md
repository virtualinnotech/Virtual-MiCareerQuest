# Student Visitors and Scooter Reward

Seven supplied student models now replace 21 walking crowd members. Seven start near the information hub; the rest join routes throughout the venue. All use the existing crowd navigation, avoidance, social stops, animation atlas and character scale. Controls > Meet new students finds an actual moving student. Clothing/hair variants tint only separate garment/hair materials; skin and facial textures remain unchanged. Original ZIPs are untouched.

The revision 102 pipeline is `rebuild102.py`, `animate102.py`, then `optimize.cjs --rigged`. Rebuild welds coincident vertices while preserving face-corner UVs, then reduces the connected geometry conservatively. Each scan retains roughly 65,000 triangles with 2048px textures. The two authored skeletons are preserved; five scans receive fitted skeletons, continuous weights and baked walk/idle clips. Faces remain rigid with their heads. Walking uses alternating planted feet, forward knee poles and arm swing. Original posed upper-body gestures are retained on the scans. Runtime `walking.js` loads `rigged102/` and uses the crowd renderer; unsafe per-UV runtime simplification is disabled for these models. The old `prepare.py` and static `visitors.js` are historical, not the active student pipeline.

The scooter and supplied off-road Segway unlock after all six missions and park beside one another at Information. T mounts the closest vehicle or dismounts; existing movement keys steer and accelerate; Shift is turbo; Space brakes and lights the rear red LEDs. Touch buttons provide turbo, braking and dismount. Movement reuses the venue swept collision solver and fixed step. Wheel rotation follows actual distance, including reverse and differential Segway turning. The rider leans smoothly with input, while hand/foot IK maintains the measured grips and deck anchors. Teleporting dismounts, and losing focus stops the ride. Both parked reward rings pulse.

`prepare-rides103.py` extracts the supplied Segway archive with path validation, repairs texture references, exports a human-scale Segway, and splits the scooter's joined tire mesh into independently rotating wheels. Original models and archives remain untouched. Its runtime outputs are `segway103.glb`, `scooter103.glb`, and `rides103-data.js` (wheel pivots/radii). `scooter.js` keeps the existing `Scooter100` API for the reward/input hooks and exposes both rides in its diagnostic snapshot.

`wayfinder103.js` uses the existing collision-aware HubGuide path and replaces the ground ribbon with floating blue orbs and pulsing raised chevrons. Swept player/orb proximity triggers persistent pops, including at turbo speed. The compass follows the player in world space and points toward the destination regardless of camera/body rotation. Changing/clearing routes resets the markers; arrival hides the compass. First-person view retains the floating path markers without a ring crossing the camera. No route GPU meshes are allocated on destination changes.

Shift+K or Complete all missions (test), available in Controls and Missions, sets an explicit local test-completion override. Alt+K remains supported in ordinary browsers, but the desktop host can intercept it before the page receives it. Shortcuts are ignored while typing in a form. Written answers and employer visit records are preserved, not fabricated. Test certificates are clearly labeled. Earned student certificates are unchanged. Progress and reward acknowledgement remain local to this browser; the parked scooter returns to the information booth after a page reload.

Tests: `node visitors100/core-test.cjs`; with Playwright available, run `walking-test.cjs`, `inspect102.cjs`, `browser-test.cjs` and `physics-test.cjs` using `NODE_PATH=../work/browser/node_modules` while the venue runs on port 8014. `fix101-test.cjs` documents the superseded stationary-group layout. Close-up face and four-phase gait captures are saved to the sibling work folder.
